Meet Joyce

Meet Joyce Baker

Sometimes, we spend so much of our lives learning how to survive that we forget how to live.

Perhaps you've become so good at holding everything together that no one realizes how much you're struggling inside. Maybe you've spent years trying to understand why you feel the way you do, why certain experiences still hurt, or why finding peace seems so difficult.

I'm Joyce Baker, originally from Brazil and an American citizen for over a decade. I hold a Master's degree in Counseling Psychology, and my clinical work focuses on trauma, anxiety, depression, OCD, and ADHD.

I believe that understanding ourselves can change the way we experience life. When we begin to understand our emotions, recognize our patterns, and make sense of our experiences, we create room for meaningful change.

My mission in this world is to guide you toward a deeper understanding of yourself, helping you live with greater emotional freedom, confidence, and purpose.

InnerStone Counseling was born from this mission. It brings together the science of psychology, the power of human connection, and the belief that each of us has the capacity to grow. Through personalized, evidence-based care, InnerStone offers a space to explore your experiences, develop emotional resilience, and build a stronger foundation within yourself.

I offer therapy in English, Portuguese, and Spanish, with a deep appreciation for the cultural and personal experiences that shape who you are.

Wherever you are in your life right now, I look forward to helping you discover what is possible from here.

Joyce Baker
